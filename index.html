FAQ

Before you send the brief.

Good data projects start with clear targeting, required fields and a defined use case. Here are the questions clients usually ask first.

Ask a question
What information can be included in a B2B lead list?+

Projects can include company name, website, industry, location, employee size, contact name, job title, business email, phone number when publicly available, LinkedIn URL and source fields depending on your brief.

Can you clean and enrich an existing CSV or Excel file?+

Yes. Existing lists can be deduplicated, standardized, verified and enriched with missing contact or company fields where they can be responsibly researched.

Do you verify email addresses before delivery?+

Email verification can include syntax, domain and mailbox-level checks depending on the project. Results can be separated into valid, risky, catch-all and invalid categories.

Do you work with public Instagram data?+

Yes. Public profile and audience research can be organized for legitimate business research, creator discovery and marketing use. Clients remain responsible for complying with applicable laws and platform rules.

What file formats do you deliver?+

CSV and Excel are the standard options. Field order, column names and status labels can be matched to your CRM or outreach workflow.
